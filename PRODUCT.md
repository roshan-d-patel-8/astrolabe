# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Astrolabe is its owner's integrated personal operating interface over an Obsidian vault. It connects whole-system orientation to specific measures and executable tasks without losing context.

## Capabilities and constraints

- Exactly five Focus Mundi altitudes: F1 Compass, F2 Cartography, F3 The Real, F4 Lens, F5 Momentum.
- Ten life and work domains span Hathi and Skoll. Domain selection carries into measures and tasks.
- TaskNotes remains canonical. This site reads an encrypted build-time snapshot of active Skoll tasks; Hathi/reminder tasks are excluded. It does not offer remote writes or continuous synchronization.
- Dashboard links open canonical Obsidian notes. Recorded refresh metadata is not proof that underlying data is current.
- Planning readings have no observation dates in the source and are not live telemetry. Do not invent trends or normalized scores.
- Personal source and snapshot are encrypted before publication. No access key or plaintext personal dataset belongs in public source control.

## Brand commitments

The Astrolabe, Axis Mundi, F1–F5, and The Real remain. The owner's explicit reference is Edward Tufte: show relationships and evidence, with precise labeling. Both light and dark modes are required. Celestial shaders and Three.js bring delight without interfering with reading and action.

## Evidence on hand

The canonical vault HTML supplies domain descriptions, value dyads, recorded readings, and questions. The existing build supplies dashboard metadata and active TaskNotes records. Task-to-domain mapping is a keyword suggestion, not a canonical assignment.
